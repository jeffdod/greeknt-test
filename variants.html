The fifty variants

The edition’s Manuscript Apparatus gives the evidence for fifty passages where the manuscripts differ in ways that matter. Here you can browse them one at a time, then see where each cited manuscript falls in history.

Draft for review. The plain-English summaries on each card are written for this page and are not from the book. The readings, manuscripts, and edition notes are taken from the printed edition.
