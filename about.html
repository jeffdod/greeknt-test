About this edition

What the book contains, how it was prepared, and how the numbers and pages on this website were made and checked.

The edition

The Greek New Testament According to the Byzantine Text was edited and prepared by J. Jeffrey Dodson and Dwayne Green for Guardian Press, with the Guardian Press Editorial Committee. It runs to 752 pages.

The main text is the Robinson–Pierpont New Testament in the Original Greek: Byzantine Textform 2026. Every change from the earlier 2005 and 2018 editions is listed in Appendix A.

Two sets of footnotes sit under the text on each page.

  • The Edition Apparatus compares the main text with eleven printed editions: Hodges & Farstad, Pickering, the Patriarchal Text, the Editio Critica Maior, Nestle–Aland 27 and 28, the SBL Greek New Testament, Tyndale House, Westcott & Hort, Scrivener, and Stephanus. Minor differences such as accents, punctuation, movable nu, final sigma and spacing in compound words are not recorded.
  • The Manuscript Apparatus gives the manuscript evidence for fifty significant variants chosen by the editorial committee. For each one it lists every papyrus and uncial extant at that point (ninth century and earlier), plus select minuscules through the tenth century.

Four appendices document the edition changes, corrections to the comparison editions, alternate readings and long variants, and a dated list of every manuscript cited.

The whole edition is in the public domain as of 16 August 2026. You may copy, print, quote, adapt and share it, even commercially, without permission or fee.

How this website was made

The book is the authority. This website is a set of tools for exploring it, and every page here was built from the printed edition.

Fifty variants

The readings, the manuscripts cited for each reading, and the edition notes come from the Manuscript Apparatus and Edition Apparatus. The short plain-English summary on each card was written for this website and is not from the book. The timeline places each manuscript by the century given in Appendix D.

Whole-text stats

Every note in the Edition Apparatus was read from the book’s PDF by software and stored as data: the book, the page and verse, the lemma, which editions share the main text, which read differently, and which print the reading in brackets or parentheses. Each note was also labelled by what the difference is (words omitted or added, word order, spelling, brackets, and so on). Those labels were assigned by rules that read the note’s wording. A single note can have more than one label, so the categories overlap, and a few notes may be labelled imperfectly.

To check the extraction, the number of times each edition’s abbreviation appears on every page was counted a second, independent way and compared with the stored data. The two agreed on every page that carries notes. That check proves the editions were captured correctly. It does not prove every label is right, so please use the report form if you find one that is not.

Page numbers on this website are the page numbers printed in the book.

Manuscripts

The Gregory–Aland number, letter, contents and date of each manuscript come from Appendix D, which takes its dates from the Kurzgefaßte Liste of the Institute for New Testament Textual Research (INTF) in Münster. The names and the places where the manuscripts are kept were compiled from public lists of New Testament manuscripts, and each card links to the manuscript’s Liste entry so you can check it against the official record. The “follows the main text” figure counts, among the fifty variants, how often a manuscript’s own reading matches this edition’s main text. It leaves out readings supplied only by a later corrector. For a manuscript cited in only a few places, the percentage can be misleading.

Fonts and design

The site sets its text in EB Garamond and its Greek in GFS Didot. Both are free fonts released under the SIL Open Font License, and they are served from this site, so no font service sees your visit.

Visitor counts

The site uses a privacy-friendly counter to tally page views and clicks on the download links. It sets no cookies and does not build profiles of visitors. The counts are private to the people who run the site.

Corrections

No edition is free of mistakes. Known corrections to the printed book are listed on the Errata page, and the same page has a form for reporting a new one, whether in the book or on this website.
