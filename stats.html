Find where

Choose an edition, a book, or a kind of difference, and see every matching note with its page number. The strip shows where in the edition the matches fall.

    An edition not named in a note reads the same as the main text. Accents, punctuation, movable nu, final sigma, and spacing in compound words are not recorded in the apparatus. Notes are printed with the readings stripped of accents, as in the edition. Page numbers are the numbers printed in the book. The kind of difference is worked out automatically by comparing the printed words. A reading that does more than one thing (for example, omits one word and adds another) is listed under each kind, so the kinds overlap and should not be added together. Treat “spelling or a small change of form” and “other wording” as a guide.
